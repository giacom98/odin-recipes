Baked Rigatoni

Home

Description:

Baked rigatoni is a classic Italian comfort dish, perfect for family dinners or gatherings. It’s made with large pasta tubes baked in a rich tomato sauce, creamy béchamel, and melted cheese. This dish combines hearty pasta with savory flavors and a deliciously cheesy crust.

Ingredients:

  • 400g rigatoni pasta;
  • 500g tomato sauce;
  • 200 ml lukewarm water;
  • 250g ricotta cheese;
  • 200g mozzarella cheese, shredded;
  • 100 g pancetta, diced;
  • 100 g Parmigiano Reggiano cheese, grated;
  • 1 medium onion, finely chopped;
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil;
  • Origano, Basil, red pepper flakes;
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste;
  • Fresh basil leaves (for garnish).

Steps:

  1. Preheat the oven to 190 degrees C.
  2. In a large pot, bring salted water to a boil. Cook the rigatoni until just al dente (about 1-2 minutes less than the package instructions). Drain and set aside.
  3. Make the sauce: in a large skillet, heat olive oil over medium heat, add the chopped onion and cook until soft (about 5 minutes). Add the minced garlic and cook for another 1-2 minutes, until fragrant. Stir in the tomato sauce, dried oregano, dried basil, red pepper flakes, salt, and pepper. Let simmer for 10 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  4. In a bowl, combine the ricotta cheese with half of the Parmesan cheese. Season with a pinch of salt and pepper.
  5. Assemble the dish.
  6. Cover the baking dish with foil and bake for 20 minutes. Then, remove the foil and bake for an additional 10 minutes, until the cheese is bubbly and golden brown.
  7. Let it cool for a few minutes before serving. Garnish with fresh basil leaves.
