Erbazzone

Home

Description:

Erbazzone is a traditional savory pie from Reggio Emilia, made with very thin pastry layers filled with Swiss chard (or spinach), Parmigiano Reggiano cheese, and pancetta. It’s delicious hot, warm, or even the next day.

Ingredients:

  • 500 g all-purpose flour;
  • 80 g lard (or extra virgin olive oil);
  • 200 ml lukewarm water;
  • 10 g salt;
  • 1 kg Swiss chard (or spinach);
  • 100 g pancetta, diced;
  • 100 g Parmigiano Reggiano cheese, grated;
  • 2 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil;
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste.

Steps:

  1. In a bowl, mix the flour and salt. Add the lard and gradually pour in the water. Knead until you get a smooth, elastic dough. Cover and let it rest for 30 minutes.
  2. Wash the Swiss chard, briefly boil it, drain well, and roughly chop it. In a pan, sauté the finely chopped onion with olive oil, add the pancetta, then the Swiss chard. Cook for a few minutes, season with salt and pepper, remove from heat, and mix in the grated Parmigiano.
  3. Divide the dough into two parts, one slightly larger than the other. Roll out the larger portion very thin and line a greased baking pan with it. Spread the filling evenly.
  4. Roll out the second sheet of dough, cover the filling, and seal the edges well. Prick the surface with a fork and brush lightly with olive oil.
  5. Bake in a static oven at 180°C / 350°F for 35–40 minutes, until the top is golden brown.
