Sausage Balls

Home

Description:

These sausage balls are always a hit, whether you're serving them for breakfast, brunch, as an appetizer at a holiday party, or as a snack during game day. The combination of savory sausage, pre-made biscuit mix, and sharp Cheddar cheese creates an irresistible flavor that's hard to beat!

Ingredients:

  • 1 pound ground pork sausage;
  • 2 cups biscuit baking mix;
  • 12 ounces sharp Cheddar cheese, shredded.

Steps:

  1. Gather all ingredients. Preheat the oven to 170 degrees C.
  2. Combine room temperature sausage, biscuit mix, and shredded Cheddar together in a large bowl; mix with your hands until well combined.
  3. Roll mixture into walnut-sized balls; transfer to a baking sheet.
  4. Bake in the preheated oven until golden brown and sausage is cooked through, 20 to 25 minutes.
